SOURCES & SCOPE

Real results. Clear boundaries.

Each borough is coloured from its official party-list second votes for the selected Abgeordnetenhaus election. Shares use valid second votes as the denominator; smaller parties are included in “Other”. The 2021 election was annulled and repeated in 2023, so its results are historical context only.

2026 results are provisional until the official final determination. Borough boundaries come from Berlin’s open ALKIS dataset. DAWUM polling is Berlin-wide only and licensed under ODbL.
